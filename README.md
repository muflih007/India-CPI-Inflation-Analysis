# India-CPI-Inflation-Analysis
India CPI Inflation Analysis using Excel, PivotTables, and Pivot Charts, including data cleaning, data processing, and missing-value imputation.

## Project Objective
The objective of this project is to analyze India’s Consumer Price Index (CPI) inflation data to understand which categories (like food, fuel, transportation, etc.) contribute most to inflation, identify trends over recent years (including before and after major events like COVID-19), and assess the impact of domestic and global factors (such as oil price changes) on overall inflation rates. The goal is to provide clear insights into inflation drivers using actual index data and visualizations, enabling better economic understanding and decision-making.

## Overall Major Findings and Insights :

* Food is Often the Largest Contributor: Analysis usually shows that food has the biggest impact on overall CPI inflation,
 especially in recent years. Food Contributes 45.8% in Overall Categories.This indicates India's inflation is mainly
 Food-driven.

* 2020 has the Highest Inflation Rate The Spike was driven by pandemic-linked supply interruption-(Fitst Lockdown),localized
food suplly stock,and temporary disruptions in distribution network leading to sharp food price-rises and volatility in other
services. Government Lockdown measures and demand supply mismatches caused shorterm spikes in certain CPI components.

*Highest Food inflation :JUNE 2022 - Lowest Food inflation : FEB 2023 ,Highest MoM increase: Around +1.04% (June 2022) — likely
due to supply-side or seasonal factors (e.g., monsoon or production cycle). Lowest MoM decline: Around -0.66% (Feb 2023) —
possibly linked to post-harvest price correction. 

* COVID-19 Caused Inflation Spikes: After March 2020, inflation rates increased sharply, particularly in categories like
healthcare, food, and essential services, due to supply disruptions and increased demand.Inflation in healthcare and
essentail rose most after COVID-19Confirms pandemic caused significant cost pressure in basic goods& services.


* Oil Prices Affect Fuel and Transport Inflation: Global oil price rises between 2021 and 2023 led to higher inflation in 
fuel and related categories in India. 

* Inflation Fluctuates Yearly: The year-on-year CPI analysis identifies the year with the highest inflation, often explained
by major events like COVID-19 or global supply issues.

* Specific Food Items Can Drive Food Inflation: Within the food category, certain items (like vegetables or cereals) often
stand out as main contributors in months with high food inflation.

These insights help policymakers focus on controlling prices in high-impact categories and understanding the effects of 
local and global events on inflation.

## Conclusion : 
The India CPI Inflation case study demonstrates that inflation in India is mainly driven by changes in food prices, global oil price fluctuations, and major events like the COVID-19 pandemic. The analysis highlights how different categories contribute to overall inflation and shows that disruptions—such as supply chain issues or increased demand—can sharply affect key sectors like food, healthcare, and fuel. Understanding these trends helps policymakers and stakeholders take focused actions to manage inflation and protect consumer purchasing power.
